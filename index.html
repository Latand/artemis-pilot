    River colors: red ship velocity · cyan space infall · violet dark-energy expansion · green Milky Way halo · yellow the nose. When cyan rivals red, the river takes the turn.

    Planet and Moon motion comes from Solar System ephemerides advanced by KDK or velocity-Verlet integration; the sim hands off to analytic osculating Kepler orbits at high warp so planets stay on their tracks. RK4 advances your ship from all Solar System bodies and player-placed holes, plus a capped, priority-ranked subset of nearby stellar sources. A separate RK4 path advances player-placed holes from Solar System bodies, gravitational debris, and other placed holes. Deep-time events such as galaxy passages and stellar eras follow reduced-order equations: simplified models with fixed parameters.

    The spacetime river is a velocity-field visualization, an honest picture of the pulls around you. Player-placed holes use the Paczynski-Wiita pseudo-Newtonian approximation. Catalog and special-object black holes, including SGR A*, use the capped active-star Newtonian field. Limited 1PN corrections cover weak-field motion near the Sun. Collisions are classified between simple spheres, with no melting or fragments. Only the Sun evolves as a star, changing its appearance and contact radius. Merger energy loss is bookkeeping; the engine carries no gravitational waveforms.

    Galaxy-scale views skip galaxy-wide N-body integration, and the Milky Way and Andromeda dates play one modeled scenario whose real outcome stays uncertain. Shift+O toggles the modeled NFW dark-matter halo; its applied strength is under physics review and currently changes little inside the galactic disk. Drag to orbit the camera; scroll to zoom. You can land on the rocky planets. Aim for the Moon, capture orbit, land, and come home, or dive into something dark.
